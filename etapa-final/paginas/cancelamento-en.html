Our policy complies with the Brazilian Consumer Protection Code (Código de Defesa do Consumidor). If the guest cancels the booking within 7 (seven) days of making it, they are entitled to a refund of 100% of the amount paid, minus any applicable credit card fees if payment was made by card.

If the guest cancels after those 7 (seven) days and up to 30 days before check-in, they are entitled to a refund of 70% of the amount actually paid. Outstanding nightly rates that have not yet been paid will not be charged.

If the cancellation takes place within the 30 (thirty) days before check-in, the guest is entitled to a refund of 50% of the amount actually paid. Outstanding amounts of the total cost of the stay will not be charged.

If the guest does not show up for check-in and gives no notice within one of the periods above, 100% of the amount actually paid will be retained and will not be refunded. Outstanding nightly rates that have not yet been paid will not be charged.
